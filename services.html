GOOD TO KNOW

Clear expectations.

How does the project begin?

We confirm the goals and deliverables in writing. Website work begins with a 50% deposit; monthly services are paid in advance. Monthly launch offers apply to month one only, then renew at the crossed-out standard price.

Do I keep my accounts and content?

Yes. Your website domain, advertising accounts and social profiles should stay in your business name. We work with partner access and hand over final approved assets.

Are taxes included in these prices?

Prices are starting fees before applicable GST. The written quote will confirm the final total and payment schedule.

Can I combine services?

Yes. We can quote a website plus ads or social handling. The quote will list every deliverable, timeline, revision and fee.
